# Journal

How the Journal tab works: writing dated entries, browsing them on a calendar, and talking a day over with the assistant.

---

## Writing Journal Entries

Open the **Journal** tab. The middle of the screen is the open day, with a box at the bottom to write in. Press **Save entry** (or Cmd/Ctrl + Enter) to save. You can save several entries on the same day; each one is shown with the time it was written.

Unsaved text stays in the box if you switch tabs or reload the page. It is kept only in your browser until you save.

You can edit an entry with the pencil button and delete it with the bin button.

## The Journal Calendar

The sidebar shows a month. Days with a dot have entries. Click a day to read what you wrote, use the arrows to move between months, and press **Today** to come back. You can add an entry to a past day by opening that day and writing in the box. Future days cannot be opened.

An entry belongs to the day it was written on in your timezone (set under the user menu, **Timezone**). Changing your timezone later does not move old entries.

## How the Journal Feeds Memory

Saved entries are added to the same memory that chat uses. A few seconds after saving, the entry shows **In memory**. From then on:

- The people, pets, places and preferences you wrote about appear in the Knowledge Graph.
- Normal chat can recall what you wrote, for example "what did I say about Dana's move?"

If an entry shows "not all of it reached memory", press **Retry**.

Editing an entry updates what is remembered from its text. Deleting an entry removes its text from memory, but facts already learned from it (for example that you have a sister called Dana) stay; remove those in the Knowledge Graph window if you want them gone.

## Talking It Over in the Journal

The panel on the right (the **Talk** switch on a phone) is a conversation about the open day. The assistant there can:

- read what you wrote that day and on other days ("what did I write last week?", "when did I last mention Sam?"),
- look things up in memory,
- read your calendar ("what's on today?") and suggest writing prompts from it,
- add an event to your Google Calendar when you ask it to.

It cannot search the web; use the normal chat for that. Its replies are not part of your journal and are not added to memory. Only saved entries are.

## Journal and Google Calendar

If Google Calendar is connected and an entry mentions a definite future plan, such as "dinner with Sam on Friday at 7", a suggestion appears under the entry with an **Add to calendar** button. Nothing is added unless you press it. Dismiss a suggestion with the x.

You can also ask in the conversation panel: "add the vet appointment on Friday at 3pm".

## Journal Privacy

Your journal is visible only to your account. Other users, including members of your groups, cannot read your entries, and the assistant never uses them in a group chat's shared memory.
